THE CONCEPT

What makes a block?

01

Color creates a surface.

A background color makes an element visually stand out. We can use it to create cards, banners, sections and buttons.

02

Dimensions create shape.

Width and height determine how much space a block occupies. A small box can become a wide banner or a large panel.

03

Space creates balance.

Padding adds space inside. Margin adds space outside. Together they help elements breathe.
